# Synthetic examples

Treat every example and scenario as a fabricated test fixture, not live market
research or a recommendation to sell a particular product in any country.
`input.json` and `output.json` show a structural success; the output assumes the
explicitly named synthetic research fixtures and cannot be reproduced by reading
only the input. `output.partial.json` illustrates partial research failure and
stricter-cap precedence. `output.error.json` illustrates no research capability.

`input.with-scout-reports.json` contains synthetic local, foreign and failed Scout
priors. `scout-report.json` shows the finalized upstream shape. The input fixtures
must not cause a real paid ad lookup. The agent still needs current ordinary web
research for a genuine product-scoring run.

`scenarios.json` records structural cases and expected behavioral decisions. Some
cases have fixture outputs; others are policy review cases, not evidence of an
end-to-end model run. Build-time checks exercise schemas and arithmetic separately.
No example supplies a live source, verified law, real market price or legal approval.
